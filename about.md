I'm **Enzo Barros Bellissimo**, founder of [Similar Info](https://similarinfo.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=referral){:rel="noopener"} (Similar Tecnologia e Inteligência Artificial Ltda, Alphaville — Barueri, SP). We build custom software and AI agents for Brazilian businesses: the first system we shipped runs an entire health-plan brokerage — sales, quoting, negotiation, onboarding and after-sales — and **Simi**, our voice agent, makes the first call to new clients.

This blog is where I write down what I'm learning along the way: what actually works in production, what breaks, and what turns out to have been the wrong problem all along.
